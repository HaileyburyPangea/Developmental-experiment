# Social, movement and emotion audit (original indices 26–51)

Reviewed all 26 records. `research-social.json` preserves one replacement per input record and the original calendar bounds; the listed bounds are often documentary compilation ages, not the inclusion criteria of a single paper. Exact timings, counterbalancing and apparatus specifications are not fabricated where the accessible source does not establish them. Procedures identify laboratory-only tasks and adapted observations.

Methods inspection: retrieved source abstracts and primary publisher records for all linked studies; inspected open peer-cooperation and slope-advice full texts and relevant methods summaries. Several publisher full texts were unavailable; those records deliberately defer exact trial counts, timing and technical specifications to the source. Unmatched observations are explicitly labelled rather than supplied with an invented citation. A later iteration can split documentary composite entries into individually scheduled, publication-specific records once their target protocol is selected.
