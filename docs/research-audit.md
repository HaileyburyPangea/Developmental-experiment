# Research and application review

All 73 original records were reviewed. Seventy now link published research or
related official evidence. Three observations (original indices 40, 42 and 55)
have no verified publication and say so in their limitations. Cards identify
experiments, observations, clinical assessments and frameworks separately.

Procedures provide materials, setup, comparisons and measurements. Some original
records combined multiple documentary clips and broad age spans. Where the exact
publication or apparatus/trial details could not be verified, the entry labels
an adapted outline or related evidence and records the remaining gap. These are
not all exact replication protocols. Research ages and illustrative calendar
slots should be distinguished when reading the limitations.

- [Language, hearing and early communication](research-language-audit.md)
- [Social, movement and emotional development](research-social-audit.md)
- [Cognition, perception and later observations](research-cognition-audit.md)

Published study methods and related evidence remain separate from scheduling
conventions. The original 12-week ages are scheduled at three calendar months
by request. The newborn language preference slot is explicitly illustrative;
the lexical/grammatical discrimination study was corrected to 1–3 days, and
pointing-referent ages to 9–12 months.

The former 30-day expiry grace period was removed. Expiry now follows the end of
the browser's local calendar day, with midnight and foreground refresh. All
studies retains historical entries. Exact hourly expiry requires a birth time,
which this date-only application does not collect. The last valid birth date is
stored locally in the browser and can be removed by clearing the date field.
