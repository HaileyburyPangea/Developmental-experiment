# Language and perception audit (original indices 0–25)

Reviewed 2026-11-01. All 26 original records are retained in `research-language.json`. Titles now describe the phenomenon instead of the demonstrator. Procedures distinguish published research descriptions from adapted observations and related paradigms. All claims are paraphrased; links lead to primary publications or primary article records. Access to an abstract is not treated as access to the complete protocol. These records provide substantially better setup, controls and measurements, but exact laboratory replication still requires original stimulus lists, scoring criteria and apparatus specifications from the paper.

Two evidence-based scheduling corrections are proposed: original index 2 from under 24 hours to 1–3 days (published participants), and index 11 from 8 months to 9–12 months (published age comparison). Other calendar slots remain unchanged; discrepancies with source ages are explicit in limitations.

## Retrieval limitations

Primary publisher/PubMed abstracts were inspected for every literature-linked topic. Some full-text opens returned access checks or restricted access. Exact unverified stimuli, trial counts and stopping criteria were therefore left explicitly unspecified instead of invented. The shape-bias and general language records intentionally avoid importing the documentary's numerical claims without a verified methods/result match.

## Additional verification

The primary publisher page https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0010027799000475 exposes a Method excerpt specifying 77 healthy newborns aged 1–3 days and 32 completers; this supports the index 2 age correction beyond its PubMed abstract. The fetal-voice abstract at https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12741744/ specifies baseline, playback and recovery periods of two minutes each; these timings are included. Index 1 display age now says “Newborn (12-hour planning slot)” to expose its scheduling approximation without silently asserting a verified 12-hour threshold.
